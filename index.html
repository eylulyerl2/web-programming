Welcome to Our Bookstore

Marginalia Books opened in 2019 in Bayrampasa. We sell new and second-hand books, including fiction, poetry and translated books. Come visit us and look around, there is no pressure to buy anything.

Our Reading Corner

We have a small reading room upstairs with a few chairs. You can sit and read any book from the shop for free, and we have tea too. It is a quiet place to relax for a while.
