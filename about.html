About This Website

This website is for our bookstore, Marginalia Books. Here you can learn about our store, see our services, and contact us. We opened on April 12, 2019 and we are located in Bayrampasa, Istanbul.

We think a bookstore should be a place where you can take your time and just look around.

Good to Know
